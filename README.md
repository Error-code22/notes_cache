**Open anything.** — A free, offline-first document reader and editor.

Foluma (formerly NotesCache) opens PDF, EPUB, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, CSV, code, images, video and audio on your device. Reading and editing work offline; Notesy, the optional AI study assistant, needs a connection.

## Features

- **Universal reader** — PDF, EPUB, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, CSV, code, images, video and audio
- **Open with / Share** — send a document from another Android app straight to Foluma
- **Note Library** — browse, search, filter, and download notes for offline reading
- **Notesy AI** — answers, study tools (flashcards, quizzes, revision plans), image support, chat history, and private vault chats
- **Communication** — chat rooms, DMs, friend codes
- **Local Docs** — open and share documents from your own device
- **Personalization** — themes, accent colors, fonts, avatars, Google Sign-In
